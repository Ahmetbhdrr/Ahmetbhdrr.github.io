Privacy Policy

3D STL Viewer : Cost Calculator · com.stlviewernova.app
Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains the data practices of the app and the third-party services used for advertising, advertising measurement and subscriptions.

Developer and contact

The app is provided by Nova Soft. Dev. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact nova.softwaredevelop@gmail.com.

3D model files and calculations

Files you open, models you process, model measurements, conversion results and cost-calculation inputs are processed on your device for the requested app functions. The developer does not upload these files or inputs to its own server. Files are saved to device storage only when you choose to save or export them.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location;
  • app interactions such as app launches, taps and ad/video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, including launch time, hangs and energy use; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID where available.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and security. Depending on your consent choice and region, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Where required by law, the app asks for consent through Google’s certified User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. The app does not request an ad until the platform reports that ads may be requested.

You can revisit, withdraw or update eligible advertising choices from Subscription → Ad privacy choices inside the app. You can also reset or delete the Android Advertising ID in Android settings. Refusing or withdrawing optional advertising consent does not prevent use of the app’s core model-viewing features.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and Advertising Policies.

Advertising measurement — Meta (Facebook) SDK

The app includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK core module so that we can measure the performance of our app-install advertising on Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram and Audience Network). The app does not include Facebook Login and does not access your Facebook or Instagram account. The Meta SDK may automatically collect and send to Meta Platforms:

  • app events such as app installs and app launches (activations);
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID where available, and an app-specific identifier;
  • technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, SDK version, language, time zone and IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.

Meta uses this information to attribute installs to ads, measure and optimise ad delivery, provide analytics, and for security and fraud prevention, as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. You can limit the use of your Advertising ID by resetting or deleting it in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you have a Facebook or Instagram account, you can also manage ad preferences in Meta’s Ad Preferences.

Subscriptions — RevenueCat and Google Play Billing

The app uses Google Play Billing and RevenueCat to offer and validate Pro subscriptions. These services process purchase history, product and subscription status, purchase tokens, an app-specific user identifier, locale/currency and related transaction information. This processing is required to complete purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, provide app functionality and measure subscription performance. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Legal basis, retention and security

Consent is used where required for personalised advertising. Contract performance and legitimate interests are used to provide app functions, verify subscriptions, secure the services and prevent fraud. Third-party providers retain data according to their own policies and legal obligations. Data transmitted by AdMob, the Meta SDK and RevenueCat is encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Use the in-app ad privacy entry point for advertising choices. For developer-held support records or help with a RevenueCat customer-data deletion request, follow the steps on our Data Deletion Request page. You may also manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

United States state privacy disclosures

The developer does not sell personal information for money. Advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and app interactions may be disclosed to Google and Meta for advertising and measurement; some US state laws may define this as “sharing” or “targeted advertising.” Where the Google privacy message makes an opt-out available, you can exercise it at any time through Subscription → Ad privacy choices. You may also contact us using the address above. The developer does not knowingly sell or share the personal information of users under 16.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

International processing and changes

Google, Meta and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than your own using safeguards described in their policies. This policy may be updated when app features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL with its effective date.
